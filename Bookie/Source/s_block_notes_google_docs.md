### SECTION B: AUDIT OF REMAINING FACTS

*(Minor NCERT factual details from Pages 291–295 that were not fully captured in the main question bank above, formatted as High-Yield Bonus One-Liners)*

1. **Storage Medium:** Alkali metals tarnish rapidly in air and react violently with water; hence, they are normally kept stored immersed under **kerosene oil** [NCERT Sec 10.1.6(i)].
2. **Proton Donors Reactivity:** Apart from water and oxygen, alkali metals react readily with other proton donors such as **alcohols, gaseous ammonia, and alkynes** [NCERT Sec 10.1.6(ii)].
3. **Thermochemical Steps for E^°:** The standard electrode potential (E^°) measuring overall reducing power is determined by three energy terms: **Sublimation Enthalpy (ΔₛᵤbH)**, **Ionization Enthalpy (ΔᵢH)**, and **Hydration Enthalpy (ΔₕydH)** [NCERT Sec 10.1.6(v)].
4. **Liquid Ammonia Amide Reaction Equation:** M⁺₍ₐₘ₎ + e⁻*{(am)} + NH*{3(l)} → MNH*{2(am)} + ½H*{2(g)} [NCERT Sec 10.1.6(vi)].
5. **Thermonuclear Reactions & Cells:** Lithium metal is specifically utilized in **thermonuclear reactions** as well as in making **electrochemical cells** [NCERT Sec 10.1.7].
6. **Organolead Additives:** PbEt₄ (Tetraethyl lead) and PbMe₄ (Tetramethyl lead) were historically anti-knock petrol additives synthesized via Na/Pb alloy, though modern vehicles use lead-free petrol [NCERT Sec 10.1.7].
