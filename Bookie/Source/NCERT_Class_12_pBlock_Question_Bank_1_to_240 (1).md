### Audit Part 2

*(Bonus high-yield one-liners covering minor factual details from Sections 7.4, 7.5, and 7.6)*

1. **Nitrogen Oxide Oxidation State Spectrum:** Nitrogen forms oxides in all formal oxidation states from **+1 to +5** (N₂O (+1), NO (+2), N₂O₃ (+3), NO₂ (+4), N₂O₄ (+4), N₂O₅ (+5)).

5. **Nitric Acid Gaseous Bond Lengths:** In planar gaseous HNO₃, the **N–OH bond length is 140.6 pm**, whereas the **terminal N=O bond length is 121 pm**, and the **O–H bond length is 96 pm**.

7. **Concentrated Nitric Acid Oxidation of Carbon:** Concentrated HNO₃ oxidises elemental carbon directly to carbon dioxide gas: **C + 4HNO₃ → CO₂ + 2H₂O + 4NO₂**.
8. **Concentrated Nitric Acid Oxidation of Sulphur:** Concentrated HNO₃ oxidises elemental octasulphur (S₈) directly to sulphuric acid: **S₈ + 48HNO₃ → 8H₂SO₄ + 48NO₂ + 16H₂O**.
9. **Brown Ring Test Ions:** The ionic stoichiometry for nitrate reduction in the brown ring test is: **NO₃⁻ + 3Fe²⁺ + 4H⁺ → NO + 3Fe³⁺ + 2H₂O**.

11. **Disproportionation of White P with Base:** The reaction of P₄ with boiling NaOH (P₄ + 3NaOH + 3H₂O → PH₃ + 3NaH₂PO₂) is a **redox disproportionation reaction** where phosphorus is simultaneously reduced to PH₃ (-3) and oxidised to NaH₂PO₂ (+1).
12. **Ignition of White Phosphorus:** White phosphorus has a very low ignition temperature and readily catches fire in air at room temperature, producing **dense white fumes of P₄O₁₀**.

14. **Thermal Stability of β-Black Phosphorus:** β-Black phosphorus does not burn in air up to **673 K**.
