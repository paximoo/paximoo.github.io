**Q11.**
* **Assertion:** The variation in properties of heavier p-block elements is more pronounced than in lighter elements.

**Q12.**
* **Assertion:** The maximum coordination number of Nitrogen in NO₃⁻ is 3, whereas Phosphorus forms PO₄³⁻ with 4-coordination.
* **Reason:** Nitrogen lacks d-orbitals in its valence shell, while Phosphorus utilizes s, p, and d orbitals to form π-bonds and expand coordination.

**Q13.**
* **Assertion:** dπ-pπ or dπ-dπ bonds formed by heavier p-block elements contribute less to overall molecular stability than pπ-pπ bonds formed by second-row elements.
* **Reason:** Valence d-orbitals are of higher energy than p-orbitals, resulting in less effective orbital overlap.

**Q14.**
* **Assertion:** Aluminium is the third most abundant element in the earth's crust by mass.
* **Reason:** Aluminium is a metal that displays chemical similarities to Boron.

**Q15.**
* **Assertion:** First ionization enthalpy values do not decrease smoothly down Group 13 from Boron to Thallium.
